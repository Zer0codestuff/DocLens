## Metrics

- **Cells**: exact text match per cell after aligning rows.
- **Numeric**: numeric cells whose parsed value matches.
- **Rows**: missing / extra rows against the truth.
- **Source**: share of compared cells whose highlighted region falls inside the true cell box.
- **Latency** includes opening the PDF and rendering. **Peak memory** is the maximum resident size of a separate process running one extraction.
